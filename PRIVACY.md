# Privacy

Vectorint keeps your budget data on your device.

## Data use

- Vectorint does not create an account.
- Vectorint does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal or financial data.
- Vectorint contains no advertising, analytics, telemetry, or tracking.
- Vectorint does not request Android's internet permission.

Your current funds, entries, recurring items, categories, tags, and settings stay
inside the app's private storage. If you export a backup, Android lets you choose
where the readable JSON file is saved. Vectorint does not upload that file.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, Vectorint asks Android for notification permission and
schedules reminders locally. Reminder notifications omit financial details from
the lock screen.

## Removing your data

You can remove Vectorint's local data through Android's app settings or by
uninstalling the app. Backup files remain wherever you chose to save them and must
be removed separately.

Questions and privacy reports can be opened in the public Vectorint issue tracker:
<https://github.com/Kamui2040/Vectorint/issues>
